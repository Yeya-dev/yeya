User guide

To start reading, import a book and open it from your library. You can set up narration, translation or reading practice whenever you need them.

Import a book

Choose Import in the library and select a file you have permission to use. Yeya reads EPUB, PDF, TXT, Markdown, MOBI and AZW3 books, as well as CBZ and CBR comics. Open the book to start reading. Yeya saves your place when you leave.

For a printed book, photograph the pages and save them as a PDF. You can read those pages in their original layout and organize the text and images later.

Adjust the reader

Open the reader controls to change the type size, font, spacing or background for reflowable text. The bottom controls include the page-turn animation. PDFs can keep their printed layout, including illustrations and page numbers.

Take notes and annotate

Select a passage to highlight, underline or circle it, add a mark in the margin, or write a note. Open your saved records to find the passage again alongside your comments and reading conversations.

To annotate with AI, describe what you want to look for, then confirm the matching rule and mark style. Yeya checks the text visible on the page. Review the reason for a mark or undo the last batch. Automatic key-point annotations have a separate switch for finding definitions, conclusions and qualifications.

Connect an AI provider and ask a question

In Settings, choose a compatible model provider, enter your own API key, select a model and test the connection. Enable the features you want to use and review the prompt for permission to send content to that provider.

In the reader, select the passage you want to discuss and ask a question. You can follow up in the same conversation. Open a source citation to check the answer against the book, or save the answer as a note.

Read in two languages

Open translation settings in the reader, select a target language and choose bilingual reading. Each translated sentence appears below the original. You can also display just the translation or return to the original text.

Adjust the translated text’s size and opacity for the book you’re reading. A glossary lets you specify preferred translations for names or recurring terms. For PDFs, the quality of the extracted or recognized text affects the translation.

Choose a voice and start listening

Start narration at your reading position. Expand the narration panel to check your progress, change the speed or set a sleep timer. You can pause and resume from the Lock Screen. Continuous narration needs text Yeya can extract from the book.

  • System voices. Choose a voice installed on your device for free single-voice narration.
  • Offline voices. Download and install a compatible voice package from the voice marketplace. It synthesizes speech on your device.
  • Online voices. Configure a speech service in the voice marketplace and preview a voice. The service receives the text it reads and may charge for usage.

For dialogue, preview voices for the narrator and each character, then change any assignments you want. Character profiles show relationships with supporting passages. Expressive delivery and reference recordings depend on the selected voice and model.

Practice from the pages you read

Choose Reading or Study for each book. Both open the original text. In Study, set the interval for practice reminders. The app records which passages appeared as you read and uses them for practice.

Choose daily, weekly or monthly exercises to work from the corresponding reading record. Yesterday’s review brings back questions you already answered. If you stop halfway through, your generated questions, draft answers and passages still due for practice are available when you return.

Store and sync your library

Books and reading records are stored on your device. To transfer them, configure iCloud, WebDAV or a supported cloud drive. Choose which categories to include and whether to upload from this device or download to it.

Set up API keys and download offline voices on each device. For details about what goes to a model or speech service and how to withdraw permission, read the privacy policy.

Free reading, the trial and purchases

Importing, basic reading, manual notes and annotations, viewing saved content and single-voice system narration are free. You can claim a 14-day trial in the app. The trial starts when you claim it and ends without an automatic charge.

An active trial, a one-time full unlock or an eligible earlier paid purchase gives you access to new AI content and advanced narration. That includes multiple character voices, expressive delivery, offline voice playback and online narration. Third-party AI and voice services bill separately.

When the trial ends, you can still read your books and saved material, including translations and study records, and save draft answers. Generating new exercises and submitting answers for grading require full access.

To restore a purchase on iPhone, iPad or Mac, sign in with the same Apple Account for Media & Purchases and choose Restore Purchases in Yeya. Restore your library separately through sync. Changing devices does not restart the trial. Regional prices appear in the App Store.
