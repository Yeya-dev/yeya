Bring a book of your own

Import an EPUB, PDF, TXT, Markdown, MOBI or AZW3 file, or open a comic in CBZ or CBR format. You can also photograph printed pages and keep them together as a PDF.

Read in two languages

Show the translation under each sentence so you can compare the wording as you read. Switch back to the original whenever you like.

Choose what to mark

Looking for definitions? Tell Yeya what to look for and choose a mark style. It can underline matching passages on the page you’re reading. You can review why it marked a passage and undo the last batch.

Listen from where you left off

Start with a voice already on your device. For a novel with a large cast, preview voices and assign them to the narrator and individual characters. Character profiles link relationships to the passages that mention them.

Adjust the speed, control playback from the Lock Screen or set a sleep timer. With full access, you can also download an offline voice package or connect an online speech service.

Practice what you just read

Choose Study for a book and read at your own pace. Yeya keeps track of the passages you’ve seen and reminds you to practice at the interval you choose.

Daily, weekly and monthly exercises draw on your reading from that period. When you return to yesterday’s questions, you can revisit the answers you actually gave.

Before you start

What can I use for free?

Import and read books, write notes, annotate by hand and listen with one system voice. Claim the optional 14-day trial in the app when you’re ready to try AI and advanced narration. It ends without an automatic charge. A one-time purchase unlocks the full app.

Do I need an AI account?

Basic reading works without a Yeya account or an API key. For AI questions, translation and new exercises, connect a compatible provider with your own key. The provider bills separately from the Yeya trial or purchase.

Where are my books and notes stored?

They stay on your device. You choose whether to sync them to iCloud, WebDAV or a supported cloud drive. Remote features ask permission before sending the relevant content to the service you select.
