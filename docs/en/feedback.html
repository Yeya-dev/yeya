Feedback and support

You can browse existing reports without signing in. To post your own, sign in to GitHub. The forms have Chinese labels and accept answers in English.

Report a problem

Include your Yeya version, device and operating system. Describe what you did, what you expected and what happened.

Ask for help

Before posting publicly

GitHub issues and attachments are public. Remove API keys, passwords, tokens and personal information from screenshots or logs. If a problem affects a private book, use a short example you wrote yourself.

Privacy and personal data requests

Email Yeya’s developer, Zhang Zhen, at 1169458576@qq.com for requests involving personal data. Include only the details needed to handle the request. The privacy policy explains how to manage permissions and delete data.
