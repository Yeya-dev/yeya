叶芽 Yeya 隐私政策

责任主体：张震 · 隐私联系：1169458576@qq.com

政策版本：2026-10-16.1 · 适用版本：叶芽 1.0

1. 适用范围

叶芽 Yeya 由张震开发与运营，本政策中的“我们”指张震。本政策说明叶芽 Yeya 如何处理个人阅读与学习数据，以及你启用可选远端功能时的数据发送。浏览官网、访问外部链接和你选择的第三方服务，也受这些服务各自的隐私政策及条款约束。开发中的功能与实际发布版本可能不同，请以所用版本中可见的功能为准。

2. 本机内容

导入的书籍文件、书库分组、阅读位置、批注、笔记、学习记录、AI 对话及生成内容首先保存在你的设备。基础导入、阅读与本机记录无需注册叶芽主账号，也不要求开启使用统计或向模型发送内容。

BYOK API Key、网盘访问令牌及 WebDAV 应用密码按用途保存在设备 Keychain；非秘密设置另存本机。凭据用于请求你选择的服务，不放入同步业务数据或应用使用事件。你主动提交给 AI 的文字若包含秘密，模型仍可能收到；请勿把密钥或密码写入问题、书籍或笔记。

3. 可选模型服务与页面图片

启用 AI 功能前，应用按接收方请求你的明确许可。模型请求可能包含你提交的问题、必要对话历史、所用功能需要的书籍正文、书名与作者、阅读事实、笔记、作答、设置或工具结果，用于阅读问答、翻译、学习内容生成、书库或记录整理等功能。今日发现只向模型发送有限书库与阅读事实；昨日总结可能使用实际已显示原文与已保存记录。已启用的自动生成和双语预取会在许可有效时发送相应内容。

自动长期记忆在本机保存有限的讲解偏好、自述基础与阅读用途、反复关注的问题和阅读节奏等观测。该功能开启且模型数据许可有效时，自动整理可能向所选模型发送最近互动的文字节选、有限回答上下文和已有观测；后续 AI 请求也可能使用相关观测。今日发现只使用有限表达偏好枚举。你可在设置关闭或清除长期记忆；清除不删除原书、笔记或历史对话，功能仍开启时可从新互动重新形成。

页面图片采用独立许可；你主动使用扫描页面识别等功能时，可能发送页面图片及提示。拒绝图片许可不等于撤回文字许可。连接测试与能力探测发送测试内容；图像能力探测可能发送应用生成的测试图片。

请求直接从设备发往你选择的官方服务或自定义地址。相应凭据用于该服务的鉴权，接收方能看到网络地址，也可能将请求内容、用量和安全日志与你的服务账户或网络信息关联。使用你自己的 Key 不会自动使远端处理匿名。

部分服务会将请求、响应或安全日志保留超过即时处理所需时间；某些免费服务也可能将输入或输出用于产品、模型或技术改进，并可能安排人工审阅。具体处理、保留期限、训练选项、部署区域和费用取决于所选服务的适用 API 条款、地区及账号配置。叶芽不承诺所有服务均不保留数据、不用于训练或采用相同的数据控制。使用前请核对服务政策，并选择你信任且具备适当数据保护措施的服务。

4. 朗读、语音输入与联网搜索

系统语音与已安装的本地声音包在设备上合成，不为合成向在线服务发送正文。下载声音包时，下载服务会收到下载请求和网络地址；这是模型文件下载，与发送书籍正文分别处理。

在线朗读在取得许可后发送待朗读文字、发音人、语速等参数，必要时使用你填写的服务凭据。Edge 朗读也是在线服务；连接测试、试听及声音查询同样可能联系相应服务。它们的处理、保留和费用遵循对应服务条款，拒绝在线许可后仍可使用可用的本地或系统声音。

语音输入由你主动开启并请求麦克风与语音识别权限，应用要求端侧识别。设备或语言不支持时可使用键盘。转录文字在设备上可编辑，之后由你发送的文字仍可能进入所选模型请求。

联网搜索单独取得许可后发送查询词给所选 Tavily 或 SearXNG 服务；查询可能根据你的问题、书名、作者或阅读事实生成。搜索服务可能将查询交给自己的搜索引擎。点击结果后访问的网站能够收到你的访问请求，并按其政策处理数据。 查询服务可能按其条款保留查询及相关日志，并将部分查询用于改善后续响应。使用服务账号时，查询也可能与该账号或网络信息关联；无 Key 或自建服务的实际处理规则应分别核对。

5. 网盘、同步与局域网传书

添加网盘或 WebDAV 连接时，你向相应服务授权访问自己内容，应用保存必要的连接资料和设备凭据。导入会请求所选目录与文件；移除连接阻止后续应用访问，但不删除第三方账号或云端文件。

数据同步默认关闭。你明确选择 iCloud 或可用的网盘/WebDAV 位置并许可后，应用可向该位置发送原书文件与结构、阅读位置、由你手动创建的笔记、书签和批注，以及学习计划与学习范围、知识掌握状态、习题、作答和批改结果、复习、测验及相关学习进度。支持同步的设置包括阅读偏好、主题、AI 功能开关、模型服务地址与模型选择、朗读选择等。AI 对话、AI 笔记、导读、书籍分析、通用章节摘要、译文、自动长期记忆、离线声音包和缓存不参与同步；用于复习的材料和摘要属于上述复习内容。API Key、密码、网盘令牌、设备权限和发送许可留在各设备，不随这些内容同步。来源不明的历史笔记不会被当作手动笔记上传。自动同步会发送后续变化；参与同步的内容删除也可能传播到其他设备。同步目的地、账号与权限由你控制，保留与删除规则同时受对应服务影响。移除连接或关闭同步不会自动删除云端副本。

iCloud 同步使用你所选 iCloud 账号的私人数据库；网盘与 WebDAV 使用你选择的账号和目的地。不同服务的访问控制、处理区域、历史版本和备份机制各不相同，应以对应服务的适用规则和你的配置为准。

Wi-Fi 传书由你主动开启，在局域网内接收文件，使用明文 HTTP。请只在可信网络中使用，并在完成后退出传书界面；同网络设备可能看到传输内容或尝试访问该入口。它不是端到端加密传输。

6. 使用统计与广告

叶芽本次首发不接入第三方使用统计 SDK，不显示第三方广告，也不请求苹果系统追踪授权。阅读时长、学习进度和 AI 用量账本属于本机功能记录，不作为应用统计发送给我们或统计服务。

这不表示整个应用没有远端数据处理。你选择 AI、在线朗读、联网搜索、网盘或同步功能时，仍会按本政策说明向所选服务发送数据，并在发送前分别请求许可。如果以后接入第三方统计或改变处理目的，我们会更新政策和相关申报，并按需要重新征求同意。

7. 控制、保留与删除

在「设置 → 数据与隐私 → 远端数据发送」查看或撤回各类许可。填写 Key、开启功能开关、完成介绍或同意使用统计不代替远端发送许可。新的接收来源或说明版本需要重新确认。撤回后应用不再发起未经授权的新请求，并停止当前模型运行和朗读；已发送的数据无法收回，远端删除请联系所选服务。 尚未开始的后续同步读写也受许可检查；已经开始或发出的请求不能保证被即时撤回。

关闭功能不会自动删除已保存的阅读与学习内容。你可删除具体书籍或记录，或使用「删除所有本地数据」；凭据在相应服务设置中另行移除。启用同步时，本地删除可能同步到其他设备。卸载前请按需备份；本机副本、系统备份与云端副本的生命周期并不相同。 删除应用中的项目也不表示同步服务中的所有历史对象、备份或其他接收方持有的副本已被删除。

本机业务内容一般由你保存至主动删除；长期记忆等派生内容可能按功能规则到期、裁剪或由你清除。第三方服务的数据保留期限、删除方法、处理区域及账号权利应以该服务当前政策与设置为准。叶芽没有权限替你删除第三方账号或保证其远端副本立即消失。

根据适用法律，你可能享有访问、更正、删除、限制处理或撤回同意等权利。请通过下方隐私邮箱提出请求；我们会以最少必要信息核对并说明可操作范围。仅留本机的内容需通过应用或设备管理；其他服务持有的数据，我们可说明相关接收方，但不能承诺越权操作。提出请求不影响你依法向适用主管机构投诉的权利。

8. 分享、官网与支持

分享图片在本机生成，由你主动调用系统分享或保存到相册。你选择的接收应用或平台适用自己的条款；系统分享完成不代表内容已在平台公开发布。主屏幕小组件读取本机 App Group 的有限阅读与记录投影，不发起模型请求，可能在桌面呈现书名或记录内容。

隐私咨询、个人数据请求和其他不适合公开的材料，请发送至 1169458576@qq.com。只提供处理请求需要的最少信息；我们会核对请求并说明可操作的范围。你发送的邮件还会经过你和我们的邮箱服务商，并受其处理规则约束。

9. 更新与联系

数据用途、接收方或重要控制发生变化时，政策及发送说明会更新，必要时重新请求同意。第三方服务可能有独立的最低年龄、使用资格、付费账号或地区要求，监护人同意不自动满足这些条件。若你是未成年人，请在适当的监护人指导下使用本机阅读功能，并只在满足所选服务条件时使用相关远端功能。监护人可就隐私问题通过下方渠道联系我们。

Yeya Privacy Policy

Operator: 张震 (Zhang Zhen) · Privacy contact: 1169458576@qq.com

Policy version: 2026-10-16.1 · Applies to Yeya 1.0

1. Scope

Yeya is developed and operated by 张震 (Zhang Zhen). “We” in this policy refers to this operator. This policy explains how Yeya handles your reading and learning data, including information sent when you enable optional remote features. Our website, external links, and third-party services you choose are also subject to those services’ own privacy policies and terms. Features under development may differ from a released version; refer to the features available in your installed version.

2. Information on your device

Imported book files, library folders, reading positions, annotations, notes, learning records, AI conversations, and generated content are stored on your device first. Basic importing, reading, and local records do not require a Yeya account, usage analytics, or sending content to an AI model.

Your own API keys, cloud-drive access tokens, and WebDAV application passwords are stored in the device Keychain for their respective purposes. Non-secret settings are stored separately on the device. Credentials authenticate requests to the services you choose and are excluded from business-data synchronization and app usage events. If you include a secret in text submitted to AI, the model service may still receive it. Do not put keys or passwords in questions, books, or notes.

3. Optional AI services and page images

Before sending AI content, the app requests your explicit permission for the recipient. Requests may contain your question, necessary conversation history, and the book text, title, author, reading activity, notes, answers, settings, or tool results needed by the feature. These support reading questions, translation, learning content, and organization of your library or records. Daily Discovery sends limited library and reading facts; Yesterday’s Reading Summary may use source text actually displayed and records you saved. Enabled automatic generation and bilingual prefetch may send relevant content while permission remains valid.

Automatic long-term memory keeps limited observations on your device, such as explanation preferences, background and reading purposes you state, recurring questions, and reading rhythm. With this feature enabled and model-data permission valid, automatic organization may send excerpts of recent interactions, limited answer context, and existing observations to your chosen model. Relevant observations may also be included in later AI requests. Daily Discovery uses only limited presentation-preference categories. You can disable or clear long-term memory in settings; clearing it does not delete books, notes, or conversation history, and new interactions can form new observations if the feature remains enabled.

Page images require separate permission. Features such as scanned-page recognition may send page images and prompts when you use them. Refusing image permission does not withdraw text permission. Connection tests and capability checks send test content; image capability checks may send app-generated test images.

Requests go directly from your device to the official service or custom address you choose. Credentials authenticate requests to that service. Recipients can see your network address and may associate request content, usage and security logs with your service account or network information. Using your own key does not automatically anonymize remote processing.

Some services retain requests, responses or security logs beyond immediate processing. Some free services may also use inputs or outputs to improve products, models or technologies and may have human reviewers examine that content. Processing, retention periods, training options, locations and charges depend on the applicable API terms, region and account configuration of the service you select. Yeya does not promise that every service avoids retention or training or provides identical data controls. Review the service’s policy before use and choose services you trust with appropriate data protection.

4. Read-aloud, voice input, and web search

System voices and installed local voice packages synthesize speech on your device without sending book text to an online service for synthesis. Downloading a voice package sends a download request and network address to the download host. Downloading model files is separate from sending book text.

With your permission, online read-aloud sends text to be spoken, the selected voice, speech rate, and other required parameters, using credentials you provide where needed. Edge read-aloud is also an online service. Connection tests, previews, and voice queries may contact the relevant service. Its processing, retention, and charges follow its terms. Available local or system voices remain usable when you refuse online permission.

Voice input starts when you choose it and requires microphone and speech-recognition permission. The app requires on-device recognition. You can use the keyboard if your device or language is unsupported. You can edit the transcript on your device; text you later submit may be included in requests to your chosen model.

Web search requires separate permission before sending queries to your selected Tavily or SearXNG service. Queries may be derived from your question, book title, author, or reading facts. The search service may pass queries to its own search engines. Websites you open from results receive your visit and process data under their own policies. The search service may retain queries and related logs under its terms and use portions of query data to improve later responses. Queries may be associated with your service account or network information. Keyless access and self-hosted services have their own processing rules.

5. Cloud drives, synchronization, and local-network transfer

When connecting a cloud drive or WebDAV service, you authorize access to your own content with that service. The app stores necessary connection information and device credentials. Importing requests selected folders and files. Removing a connection prevents subsequent app access but does not delete the third-party account or its remote files.

Data synchronization is off by default. After you select iCloud or an available cloud-drive/WebDAV destination and give permission, the app may send original book files and structure, reading positions, notes, bookmarks and annotations you created manually, study plans and study scopes, knowledge state, exercises, answers and grading results, reviews, quizzes and related study progress to that destination. Supported settings include reading preferences, themes, AI feature switches, model-service addresses and model choices, and read-aloud preferences. AI conversations, AI notes, chapter openings, book analysis, general chapter summaries, translations, long-term memory, offline voice packages and caches do not participate in synchronization. Materials and summaries used for review belong to the review content described above. API keys, passwords, cloud-drive tokens, device permissions and sending permissions remain on each device and do not synchronize with this content. Historical notes of unknown origin are not treated as manual notes for upload. Automatic synchronization sends later changes; deletion of participating content may propagate to other devices. You control the destination, account and access permissions. The service also determines retention and deletion. Disconnecting or turning off synchronization does not automatically delete remote copies.

iCloud synchronization uses the private database of your selected iCloud account. Cloud-drive and WebDAV synchronization use the account and destination you select. Access controls, processing locations, version history and backup mechanisms differ between services and depend on the applicable service rules and your configuration.

You explicitly start Wi-Fi book transfer to receive files over your local network. It uses unencrypted HTTP. Use it only on a trusted network and leave the transfer screen when finished. Devices on the same network may observe transferred content or try to access the transfer endpoint. This transfer is not end-to-end encrypted.

6. Usage analytics and advertising

This launch of Yeya does not include a third-party usage-analytics SDK, display third-party advertising, or request Apple’s tracking permission. Reading time, learning progress and the AI usage ledger are local feature records. They are not sent to us or an analytics service as app analytics.

This does not mean that the whole app has no remote processing. If you choose AI, online read-aloud, web search, cloud-drive access or synchronization, data is still sent to the selected service as described in this policy, with separate permission before sending. If we later introduce third-party analytics or change processing purposes, we will update this policy and the relevant disclosures and request consent again when required.

7. Controls, retention, and deletion

View or withdraw permissions in Settings → Data and Privacy → Remote Data Sending. Entering a key, enabling a feature, completing the introduction, or agreeing to analytics does not replace remote-data permission. A new recipient origin or disclosure version requires confirmation again. After withdrawal, the app does not start new unauthorized requests and stops the current model run and read-aloud. Previously sent data cannot be recalled; contact the chosen service about remote deletion. Subsequent synchronization reads and writes that have not started also check permission. Requests already started or sent cannot be guaranteed to be immediately recalled.

Turning a feature off does not automatically delete saved reading or learning content. You can delete individual books or records, or use “Delete all local data.” Remove credentials separately in the relevant service settings. When synchronization is enabled, local deletion may propagate to other devices. Back up data as needed before uninstalling; device copies, system backups, and remote copies have different lifecycles. Deleting an item in the app does not mean that all historical objects, backups or copies held by a synchronization service or another recipient have been deleted.

Local business content is generally kept until you choose to delete it. Derived data such as long-term memory may expire, be reduced, or be cleared under the feature’s rules. Third-party retention periods, deletion procedures, processing locations, and account rights follow those services’ current policies and settings. Yeya cannot delete their accounts for you or guarantee immediate removal of remote copies.

Depending on applicable law, you may be entitled to request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, or withdrawal of consent. Contact the privacy address below. We will verify the request with the minimum information needed and explain what we can handle. Content remaining solely on your device must be managed through the app or your device. If another service holds the data, we can identify the relevant service but cannot promise to perform actions outside our authority. Requests concerning applicable rights do not remove any rights you may have to complain to the relevant authority.

8. Sharing, website, and support

Share images are generated on your device. You choose when to use system sharing or save them to Photos. Recipient apps and platforms apply their own terms; completion of system sharing does not prove that content was publicly posted. Home Screen widgets read limited local reading and record snapshots through the App Group, do not make model requests, and may display book titles or record content on your Home Screen.

The website consists of static GitHub Pages with no analytics, login, or payment added by us. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may record visits and network information under its own policy. Public feedback uses GitHub Issues. Submitting an issue requires GitHub login; issues, usernames, comments, and attachments are publicly visible. Do not submit private books, API keys, passwords, personal data, or sensitive logs there.

Send privacy questions, personal-data requests, and other material unsuitable for public posting to 1169458576@qq.com. Provide only the information necessary to handle your request. Correspondence you send also passes through your and our email providers and follows their processing rules.
