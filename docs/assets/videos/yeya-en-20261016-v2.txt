Yeya promotional video English script

01 Yeya reader

Yeya is a reader for books you import yourself. Adjust the page to suit you, make notes, or carry on listening.

02 Import your own books

Import EPUB, PDF, text, Markdown, or supported Kindle files. Use Files, send books over Wi-Fi, or connect a cloud drive or WebDAV.

03 Adjust the page

Choose the font, size, spacing, and background you prefer. You can turn pages or scroll, depending on how you like to read.

04 Annotate as you read

Mark a sentence with a highlight, underline, or circle, and add a note. Open that record later to return to the passage.

05 Listen from where you stopped

When you want a break from the screen, open the player and listen from your current place. It carries on into the next chapter.

06 Three sources for voices

Choose a voice from your device, install an offline voice pack, or connect an online speech service.

07 Offline voice packs

Kokoro and VITS are among the adapted offline voice packs. Download from ModelScope or Hugging Face, then listen without a connection.

08 Online speech services

Online options include Edge, OpenAI, MiniMax, Doubao, and Azure. You can also configure a compatible custom endpoint.

09 Speed and sleep timer

Change the speed or voice, and set a sleep timer when you need one. Playback continues with the player closed or the screen locked.

10 Translation beneath the original

Turn on bilingual reading for a book in another language. The translation appears beneath the original, so you can compare a sentence while you read.

11 Ask about what you're reading

Ask about a sentence you don't understand. Follow up on the explanation, request a more natural translation, or change how a term is worded.

12 "Make the text larger"

Tell the AI you want larger text, more space between lines, or a warmer background. It changes the reader settings and reports what changed.

13 "Underline this sentence"

Ask the AI to underline a sentence in the book, like the green wavy line here. You can change the style or color, or add a note.

14 Organize your library

For a growing library, ask the AI for a folder plan. Check where each book would go, edit the folder names, and apply the plan.

15 Find a passage or note

If you only remember part of something, ask the AI to find it. It can look for a book, a passage, or a saved annotation or note.

16 Summarize your reading records

Your records keep annotations, notes, and AI conversations together. Ask for a summary of one topic and save it as a note, with links to its sources.

17 Change settings by asking

Ask for a daily reading goal of thirty minutes, or tell the AI how to set your reminders. It changes settings directly and opens fields you need to fill in yourself.

18 Choose how to read each book

Each book can have a different goal. Read a novel for pleasure, study a practical book to use its ideas, or prepare for an exam. Tell the AI what you want.

19 Plan reading and study

The AI uses your goal and daily available time to plan reading or study. The Today page shows what to work on and the estimated time.

20 Work through a difficult idea

For a difficult idea, ask for another explanation or an example. Try explaining it in your own words, then use the feedback to see what you missed.

21 Check your understanding

Exercises let you check what you understood. Read the feedback after answering, and return to the supporting passage in the book when you need another look.

22 Review and quizzes

Review what you have studied, or take a quiz. The AI uses your saved answers and understanding checks to help you decide what to revisit.

23 Yeya

Yeya. Open a book and pick up where you left off.
